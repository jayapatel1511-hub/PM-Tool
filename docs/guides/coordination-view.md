# Discipline coordination view

One view of what a discipline owes, waits for, uses, must re-check and can start. It sits at the top of Project →
**Weekly Coordination**, and across your projects in **My Work → Coordination**. Back to the
[user guide](../user-guide.md).

The view reads the registers; it changes nothing. Marking a meeting reviewed does not accept a handoff, approve a
review, close an assessment or sign a promise.

## Who can do what

| Action | Who |
|---|---|
| See the view | Anyone who can see the project. Restricted projects appear only to their members |
| **Capture action** in meeting mode, **Mark as reviewed** | PM or any discipline lead |
| Save a personal view | Anyone who can see the project |
| **Share with everyone on this project** | PM or any discipline lead |

## The five questions

| Card | Shows |
|---|---|
| **What do we owe?** | Outgoing handoffs not yet incorporated or cancelled, with their promised (or needed) date |
| **What are we waiting for?** | Incoming handoffs that are Submitted, Clarification Requested, Returned or Accepted |
| **Which revision are we using?** | How many input uses are recorded; open **Changes** for the list |
| **What changed?** | Open change notices, with the number of pending assessments |
| **What can we start?** | Work with a readiness assessment that is due in the window, for example "2 Ready of 5 assessed", with each item's blocking and unknown checks. Work never assessed is not counted |

Below the cards, **Issues linked from reviews** lists issues raised from current review findings, and a second **What are
we waiting for?** list groups each unaccepted handoff with its receiving task and every task that depends on it. Each row
opens its record; **Open register** opens the full list. Lists show the first four items; the count is the full number.

## Use it in a project

1. Project → **Weekly Coordination**.
2. Choose the discipline in the discipline selector (**All disciplines** or one). It is hidden in meeting mode, so choose
   it first.
3. In **Discipline coordination**, narrow by **Owner** (**All permitted owners** or a person), **From** and **To**.
   **Clear scope** resets them.

- With **All disciplines**, the handoff cards follow you, or the owner you choose. With a discipline, they follow that
  discipline's sending or receiving side.
- Choosing a discipline also limits revision uses, changes, reviews and linked issues to that discipline's work, even
  though the cards and the note under the title still say "project-wide".
- **From** and **To** filter handoff dates and the due dates of assessed work only.
- "What can we start?" covers the coordination window (3 weeks by default; your administrator sets it) unless you choose
  dates.
- "Client refreshed at …" is when the figures were read.

## Capture a meeting action

1. Select **Meeting mode**.
2. On a change notice in **What changed?**, or on a handoff group under the second **What are we waiting for?**, select
   **Capture action**.
3. In **Meeting action**, keep **Today's coordination meeting** or choose a recorded meeting, write the **Action**, choose
   its owner and **Due** date, and select **Add action**.

The action links to the handoff or change notice and to the affected work; its panel lists them under **Linked items**.
An open action already linked appears as "Existing action: …". Choose **Reuse existing action**, select an open action,
and confirm to add the missing source and affected-work links without sending another action notice. Choose
**Create separate action** only when a separate assignment is needed.

## Across your projects

**My Work → Coordination** shows the same questions for each permitted project in the project selector's scope
(Archived and Cancelled projects are left out).

- Filters: **Project** (**All permitted projects** or one), **Discipline** (**My project disciplines** uses your primary
  discipline on each project), **Owner** (**All owners**), **From**, **To**.
- **Export these evaluated records as CSV** downloads exactly the records on screen, with the time they were evaluated.
- **Print this view** prints without the controls.
- **Open project meeting mode to assign or update an action** opens that project's meeting mode with the same scope.

## Saved views

1. Set the filters, then **Views → Save current view…**.
2. Enter a **Name**. Optionally tick **Open this list in this view by default** or, on a project view as PM or lead,
   **Share with everyone on this project**.
3. Select **Save**.

A view stores the filters, not the rows, so it always shows current data. On **Weekly Coordination** it keeps the
discipline, owner and dates; on **My Work → Coordination** it also keeps the project scope. Pick it again from **Views**.
