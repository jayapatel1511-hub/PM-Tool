Tuesday: staffing signals, trustworthy counts and a calm workspace (packets 036 and 037)

## What changed

This branch brings the Tuesday app up to release candidate `ceedcce565a90dd3860e38132487d7ba8d77421a`.

**Staffing and signals (packet 036)**
- **Open roles.** PMs can staff a project with a role before anyone is named. Roles show on the Allocations tab and in Workload under Unfilled roles. They are filled through the usual allocation proposal, and a decline or cancel reopens them.
- **More link types.** Start-to-Start and Finish-to-Finish links join Finish-to-Start, with a lag in working days, on tasks and deliverables.
- **New attention rule.** "Status not moving" (A-21) flags work whose status has stood still for 15 days while people are still working on it.
- **Grouping.** People get a home discipline. Workload and the Weekly Planner can group people by Reports to, Office, Job title or Home discipline.
- **Free hours in the picker.** The person picker shows free hours and time away for the task's week, to people who can already see that capacity.
- **Counts and timelines.** Every dashboard tile opens exactly the rows it counts and explains itself. Timelines say which dates they draw and drop nothing.

**Calm workspace (packet 037)**
- Work appears near the top of every screen. Filters sit in one Filters popover, project pages have at most 7 tabs plus More, and the sidebar has at most 8 items.
- Headers fit on one line, with one signal per row and fewer colours. My Work opens on Today.
- Nothing was removed; everything is at most two clicks away.

**Also on the branch**
- **Earlier Tuesday work.** The visual standard, the weekly planning layer (packet 034) and availability with the weekly calendar (packet 035).
- **Fix 036-D18.** The demo seed now gives its In Progress work a start date.
- **Opt-in review data.** A `--with-staffing-fixtures` flag on the hosted review fixture wrapper seeds the packet 036 review data. It is off by default.
- **Two additive migrations.** `AvailabilityAndCalendarFeeds` and `StaffingSignals`.
