## Build 051-S01

Autosave hook and settings behind `flag.data_entry_safety` (declared in `Flags.cs`). `useAutosaveField` in `web/src/components/hub/fields.tsx` takes the task panel's per-field commit, and every inline edit-panel field (task, deliverable, risk, issue, decision, action and milestone panels) goes through it. With the flag on, text fields also save after `autosave_delay_ms` without typing, and leaving the field does not send that value again; with the flag off, fields save on blur exactly as before (the same `track(onSave(value))` call). Saves from one field go one at a time: a pause or blur save made while that field's previous save is out waits until the saved value is back and then goes out from the newest render, so it carries the new row version; a refused or failed save releases the field at once, and Escape drops a waiting value. Text fields whose change needs a reason (a Complete project) save on blur only, so the reason is asked once (FR-SAV-01). New organisation settings `autosave_delay_ms` (800, 200 to 10000) and `draft_keep_days` (7, 1 to 365) in a "Data entry" group, listed in Admin settings and sent in `/me` only while the flag is on. `en.ts` gains the group and two setting labels; `openapi.json`, `api.d.ts` and `tests/Hub.Tests/Snapshots/shapes.json` gain the two `/me` fields. Covers FR-SAV-01, FR-SAV-07, FR-SAV-08.
