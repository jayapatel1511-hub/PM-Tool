## Scope and non-negotiable invariants

- Exactly **two new private tables**, NoticeDeliveryIntent and NoticeDeliverySlot. Existing UserSetting/EmailMessage gain nullable additions. No attempt table, generic message bus, new role, delegate delivery or email tracking is introduced. Private entities are never registered as business audit/exports or returned directly to the product.
- people_screens owns enhanced delivery/preferences/reminders. Legacy unlinked mail retains its existing path and old wire. A linked or partially linked add-on email never falls through to that path when the flag is off or metadata is malformed.
- Recipient, source occurrence/content/scope, original suppression anchor, slot schedule/window and queued payload/membership are immutable. Only disposition, counters, reservation pointer and the current email claim/outcome are mutable. Claims exist **only on EmailMessage**.
- All policy-sensitive capture/reservation/claim and old channel/digest/follow preference writers use one recipient transaction-lock convention. An enhanced preference revision prevents stale fragment saves; it is not a cached permission or delivery authorization.
- An occurrence is consumed only by its explicit rendered held-line membership and linked accepted payload. Same item, ordinary digest row, cap/overflow or queue timestamp is not inclusion evidence. Membership may be grouped only when the rendered line explicitly accounts for those exact occurrences.
- At most **3 compositions** per digest slot, initial plus2 safe pre-send recompositions, and **8 sender-attempt reservations total** per logical slot. Neither counter resets with a new composition/email. Uncertain/in-flight payloads are never recomposed or released as safely unsent.
- Actual SMTP/Graph acceptance, Log simulation, unknown transport, failure, in-flight uncertainty and exhaustion stay distinct. No legacy SentAt/EmailedAt/DigestIncludedAt or current configuration proves actual email. Provider acceptance is not inbox delivery or a read receipt. All verification uses fakes; no test invokes an external mail provider.

Checks: enum membership; nonempty occurrence/event/suppression keys; valid item pair; a nonnull app link requires a positive captured ordinal, and an ordinal is null or positive; distinct nonempty scoped IDs at helper validation; nonnull creation/update times. Cross-recipient app/email/slot links must also be rejected by every capture/compose/claim/read helper; a single-column FK alone does not establish recipient equality.

## Locking, capture and typed claims

1. **One lock key:** transaction advisory lock `pg_advisory_xact_lock(hashtext('notice-recipient:' + canonical lower-case Guid-D))`, passed as a bound parameter. Every participant uses the exact same namespace/format. When a business command captures several recipients, acquire all distinct keys in ordinal Guid-D order before processing any. Slot/domain/intent updates occur only after this lock. JobHost's per-job lock is additional, not a substitute.
2. **Commit with the originating work:** enhanced capture joins the existing business transaction and saves app notification/intent/eligible immediate queue atomically with it. It never independently commits inside Notifier. Root ensures the hook has an outer Tx.Run boundary; sites without one must be wrapped by root, not partially committed by D. Use lock-first ReadCommitted policy reads, the current default Tx.Run behavior. A pre-lock repeatable-read snapshot or tracked stale preference entity cannot establish current opt-out state; incompatible originating transactions require root integration before admitting capture.
3. **Existing writers participate:** event-channel PUT/DELETE, digest PUT, follow PUT/DELETE, enhanced notice PATCH and their first-row GetOrCreate work run under that recipient key, with fresh policy reads after locking. Resolve the effective post-save defaults/follow behavior: DELETE is not assumed to enable mail or mean Muted. Wire/statuses and old user-field updates stay exact; locks do not create an unnecessary settings row. Enhanced revision protects only its own fragment. Definite channel/mute opt-out terminal-suppresses affected safely unsent linked intents under the same lock, preserving the captured actual direct-recipient exception under the canonical event rule. Later opt-in does not revive them. An observed access/recipient refusal similarly persists terminal suppression. In-flight payloads instead retain recorded uncertainty; bytes cannot be recalled.
4. **Rolling suppression:** after current no-self/active/access/channel/address/mute checks, serialize the existing event/item/recipient domain. Search eligible legacy queued/sent mail and enhanced original anchors with `anchor >= now−24h`; the exact24h boundary is still suppressed, matching current Notifier. Held counts in the same domain. Duplicate creates no new anchor, email or expiry extension. Retry/release keeps occurrence, content and original anchor; do not count a recomposed digest envelope or duplicated suppressed row as a fresh immediate-work anchor.
5. **Proposed claim DTOs:** `NoticeSendClaim(EmailId,Token,Version,ClaimedAt,TransportKind,UserId,SlotId?,CompositionVersion?,RenderedOccurrenceIds,immutable envelope)` and `NoticeClaimResult(Decision,Claim?,ReasonCode?)`. Internal claim purpose distinguishes initial, known-failure retry and explicitly admitted same-payload uncertain retry. Caller cannot supply another user/scope or promote an arbitrary row version. Policy-declined results contain no send envelope.
6. **Claim transaction:** lock recipient; reload actual feature flag/current preferences/follow exceptions, active recipient/current canonical address, exact project/planning scope and canonical day capacity. Unknown capacity is PendingAvailability. Under a valid current slot/pointer/version and remaining budget, increment counters, generate the email claim triple, capture the actual sender kind, set InFlight and commit. Sender I/O runs outside that transaction using only the immutable claim envelope. A stale/foreign membership or changed recipient address is not rewritten in place. Pre-send unsafe digest requests Recompose only when it has no uncertainty; unsafe immediate work is terminal-suppressed.
7. **Complete transaction:** reacquire the same recipient lock, condition on email ID/token/version/current composition and intent email pointer. Commit actual outcome/timestamps and only exact membership transitions. A stale completion cannot overwrite a newer claim/result or consume released membership. Successful declared Smtp/Graph is Accepted; Log is Simulated; successful undeclared sender is Unknown and cannot prove external delivery. Definite pre-acceptance failures are Failed; lost/ambiguous acceptance or an orphaned claim is Uncertain with sticky AcceptanceUncertainAt. At limit, definite failure is Exhausted; an uncertain budget-exhausted payload remains uncertain with no retry budget, not “not sent”.
8. **Recovery never changes payload:** no timeout or process restart authorizes a new composition/release of in-flight/uncertain membership. Only explicitly admitted current-policy retry of the same immutable email can obtain a new email claim/version within the remaining budget; possible external duplication remains visible. A different registered transport, altered content or new recipient is not that retry. An already real-accepted payload is never retried. Simulated/unknown terminal history is not replayed automatically when configuration later becomes real.

## Window, rendered membership and safe recomposition

- At slot creation, persist WindowThrough=the captured UTC composition cutoff and WindowFrom=the normal since-watermark captured before LastDigestAt preparation updates. If no prior watermark exists, use preceding24h for Daily or7days for Weekly. The watermark is preparation bookkeeping, not actual-transport proof. A safely unsent exhausted prior slot retains its earlier From as carry-forward coverage for the next eligible slot; never treat an uncertain slot as safely unsent carry-forward.
- Every recomposition uses that same immutable From/Through. Ordinary update sections use `From < occurredAt <= Through`; held candidates use pending eligible occurrences at/before Through, including older held overflow from before From. Newer occurrences wait for a later slot. Dated sections reuse canonical Digest.Build rules/day inputs and current authorized records; this does not claim reconstruction of historical entity state.
- Extend existing Digest.Build through a bounded enhanced-only slot overload/core parameter; do not copy its section policy or change the legacy off signature/path. The composer returns body/required scope and explicit rendered held-line occurrence IDs. An ordinary same-item row consumes no held ID. A grouped line states its real included occurrence count/IDs. Cap/“and n more” and omitted members reserve/consume nothing.
- Queue composition1 and set the exact rendered intents to ReservedForDigest/DeliveryEmailId in one transaction; persisted email membership is immutable. Confirm only those IDs on matching real acceptance. Simulated/unknown outcomes remain separately labelled and are never actual-mail inclusion proof. No observed result is derived from today's provider configuration.
- If access is revoked before any possibly accepted send, suppress the old email, terminal-suppress revoked intents, release allowed exact reservations and compose the same slot's next version. Preserve original window and the normal content that a changed LastDigestAt would otherwise omit. Revalidate all required projects and planning scopes; do not blindly append stale bodies. There is no new logical weekly slot on Tuesday.
- At composition3 exhaustion, safely release still-allowed entries for the next eligible slot and retain safe window coverage; record Exhausted without a hot loop. At8 sender reservations no new send is admitted. A prior AcceptanceUncertainAt or unresolved InFlight prevents any safe release/recomposition even if a later failure is definite. Counter/state checks are performed under the recipient lock, not merely in a scanner's initial query.

| Condition | Persisted/effective transition |
|---|---|
| Eligible work, known Normal>0 && Available0, hold on | HeldAway; no immediate sender call. Reduced>0, zero-normal and nonworking are not away under §42. Unknown/missing capacity is PendingAvailability, never0. |

| people_screens off | No new linked capture, reservation, claim, composition or reminder scheduling. Existing linked additions are dormant and retained; unlinked legacy path remains exact. | Email channel/mute/access/current recipient invalid | Distinct ChannelOff/Muted/AccessRemoved/RecipientInactive/NoAddress. Persisted terminal suppression never revives on later opt-in. | Ordinary Weekly digest due | Create only on Monday at/after selected organisation-local time. Monday-away/not eligible creates no slot and keeps the window/holds for next eligible Monday. Retry/recomposition of an already-created Monday slot can occur later; no new Tuesday catch-up slot. WeeklySummary is unchanged. |
| In-app self reminder due | Active opted-in user only; unique user/local-week slot independent of edits, no Intent/email, no quota/other-person hours/deputy/global no-self exception. Atomic own notification plus Emitted slot. Link preserves Time/ReadOnly guards. |

Reminder weekday/time resolve in Clock.Zone(org settings), retaining its UTC fallback for invalid zone IDs. A nonexistent local time advances to the first valid instant after the gap; ambiguous time uses the earlier UTC instant, with the weekly unique slot preventing the later repetition. Only the current local week is eligible; no historical catch-up. Changing weekday/time after Emitted cannot fire again that week. Digest slot basis is captured once; schedule edits cannot create a second slot for the same local date. A new one-minute reminder IJob is proposed; root registers it only after the exact source freeze. No reminder is routed through IEmailSender.
